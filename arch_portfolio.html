About Me

Shaping Spaces,
Inspiring Minds

I am a Professor of Architecture and Urban Design with over two decades of experience bridging the gap between academic theory and built practice. My work explores the intersection of sustainable design, cultural heritage, and contemporary urbanism.

As an educator, I believe architecture is not merely the design of buildings — it is the design of human experience. My research focuses on how built environments shape communities, particularly in rapidly urbanizing contexts.

I have led design studios, directed research centers, and collaborated with international institutions across the Middle East, Europe, and Asia.

Journal Article · Q1 Indexed · 2024
Bioclimatic Strategies in Contemporary Egyptian Residential Architecture
Published in: Sustainable Cities and Society, Elsevier
DOI: 10.1016/j.scs.2024.00892 | Citations: 14
This paper examines passive cooling strategies derived from vernacular Egyptian architecture and their integration into contemporary residential design, demonstrating up to 32% reduction in thermal loads.
